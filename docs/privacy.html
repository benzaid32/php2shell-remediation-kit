Privacy notice

Last updated: 21 October 2026

PHP2Shell Response (“we”, “us”) provides independent PHP remediation and patch verification. This notice explains how we handle personal data collected through this website, the contact form, and the free remediation kit.

What we collect

  • Contact details you submit: name, work email, company, website, number of PHP hosts, timeline, and message.
  • Correspondence and call notes needed to respond to your enquiry or deliver a service.
  • Basic technical logs from our hosting provider, such as IP address and request metadata.

Do not send passwords, tokens, customer data, or third-party host details. We do not ask for them through this website.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and scope a possible engagement (legitimate interests and pre-contractual steps).
  • To deliver the free kit or a paid service you request.
  • To keep records of consent, opt-outs, and contractual obligations.
  • To comply with applicable law.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We may use service providers for email, scheduling, hosting, and customer records under appropriate terms. If you enable a third-party form endpoint, name that processor here before collecting submissions.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 12 months unless a contract is signed. Contract and tax records are kept for the period required by law. Suppression records are kept to honour opt-outs.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to request portability. Contact us at the contact address on the main page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

If a service provider processes data outside your region, we use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies or run analytics scripts. Our hosting provider may process request logs under its own privacy terms.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top is the effective date. Material changes will be posted on this page.
